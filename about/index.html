Quran, namaz and study, in one calm app

Prayer times, Qibla, the Quran, your namaz record and a study planner. It works offline. No ads, no tracking, and your records stay on your phone.

Open the web app Android app: in testing

What is inside

Quran

Arabic text from the Tanzil Project, unmodified and checked against the original source by automated tests. Urdu translation by Maulana Fateh Muhammad Jalandhari. Recitation audio, bookmarks and a khatm plan.

Namaz times and Qibla

Prayer times for your city, worked out on the phone without the internet. Monthly timetable, jamaat times, a Qibla compass and the Hijri calendar.

Namaz record

Mark each namaz, keep count of qaza, and use the tasbih counter. Reminders that you choose.

Study

A focus timer, a weekly timetable and a report of your study time.

Learn

A Quran quiz and revision cards for hifz.

Your data stays yours

Namaz, Quran and study records are kept only on your phone. No ads and no analytics. An account is optional.

Languages

English, Hindi, Hinglish, Urdu and Arabic.

Sources

Quran text: Tanzil Project (tanzil.net), unmodified. Urdu translation: Maulana Fateh Muhammad Jalandhari, digital text from Tanzil. City locations: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Magnetic declination for the Qibla compass: World Magnetic Model, NOAA. The full list is in the app under More → About & sources.
