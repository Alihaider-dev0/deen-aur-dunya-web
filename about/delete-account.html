Delete your account

Deen aur Dunya works fully without an account. If you created the optional e-mail account, you can delete it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open More → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.
  3. If the app asks, enter the 6-digit code we e-mail you (this proves it is really you).

This deletes your login account on our server, clears everything the app saved on your phone and signs you out.

Without the app

Send an e-mail from the address you signed in with to deeenaurdunya@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done. We may ask you to confirm with a code first, so nobody can delete someone else's account.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your e-mail address, your sign-in sessions, and the login log entries that name you (they hold your e-mail and IP address).
  • On your phone (when you delete in the app): your namaz record, Quran reading positions and bookmarks, study data and settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Your namaz, Quran and study records were never uploaded, so there is nothing to keep.
  • Our login provider (Supabase) keeps its own short technical request logs, which include IP addresses, for 1 day on our current plan (7 days on its paid plan). They are deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports, if you allowed them, contain no account details and are deleted automatically within 90 days.

Only want to clear the phone and keep using the app without an account? Use Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data.
