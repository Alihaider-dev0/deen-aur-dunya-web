Privacy policy

Deen aur Dunya is a Quran, namaz and study app. It works offline. Most of what you do in it never leaves your phone.

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Contact: deeenaurdunya@gmail.com

1. What stays on your phone only

  • Kept only on your phone: your namaz record, qaza and tasbih counts; Quran reading position, bookmarks, reading minutes and khatm plan; study subjects, sessions, notes and timetable; your settings.
  • Your city and GPS location are used on the phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. They are not sent to us.
  • No cloud backup: the app turns off Android cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer for its data.
  • Moving to a new phone: use Settings → "Copy my data" and "Restore my data".

2. What can leave your phone, and why

Optional account (e-mail login)

If you choose to sign in, your e-mail address is used to send you a one-time code and to identify your account. It is stored by our login provider, Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India). Your namaz, Quran and study records are not uploaded.

Crash reports (optional)

If the app breaks, a report may be sent so we can fix it. It contains the error type and message, where in the code it happened, the app version, the screen name, the app language and basic device details (phone model, Android version, free memory and storage). It also carries a random installation number made for crash reports; this is not your advertising ID. Before an error from the app itself is reported, e-mail addresses, web addresses, numbers, quoted text and non-English text are removed from its message. A crash inside Android or a plugin is reported as Android records it. Reports are not meant to carry your namaz, Quran or study records, your e-mail or your location. They are processed by Google Firebase Crashlytics. You can turn this off at any time: Settings → Privacy → "Send crash reports". Turning it off also deletes reports still waiting on the phone.

Quran recitation audio

Recitation is streamed from everyayah.com when you press play. That site receives your IP address, like any website you visit.

App settings file

The app downloads a small, signed settings file from our website (the Hijri date adjustment and whether the optional account is open). No personal data is sent.

3. What we never do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone recordings or messages.

5. How long data is kept

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it (Settings → "Delete all my data") or uninstall the app.
  • Your account e-mail is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it also deletes the login log entries that name you.
  • Our login provider's technical request logs (with IP addresses) are deleted automatically after 1 day on our current plan (7 days on its paid plan).
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.

6. Deleting your data and account

  • On the phone: Settings → Privacy → "Delete all my data".
  • Account: Account → "Delete my account". This deletes your login account on the server, clears the phone and signs you out.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on Delete your account, or e-mail us from your account address with the subject "Delete my account".

7. Children

Everything works offline without an account, and that needs no personal data. The optional cloud account is meant for adults (18+). It will not be offered to users under 18 until a parent can give verifiable consent, as Indian law (DPDP) requires.

8. Security

All connections use HTTPS. The app's code is checked for leaked secrets and vulnerable libraries before each release. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong we will inform affected users as the law requires.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app changes what it collects, and change the date above. Questions or requests: deeenaurdunya@gmail.com.
